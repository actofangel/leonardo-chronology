## Conflicts found (sources vs sources, sources vs draft)
* **po-leo10:** sources disagree on the election.
  * 9 March 1513 (Britannica, Tudor Society) against 11 March, morning scrutiny (1513 conclave article). Both are recorded as disputed alternatives.
  * The proclamation was on 11 March and the coronation on 19 March.
  * The ordination as priest and bishop is dated 15 and 17 March, or 17 and 19 March, depending on the source.
  * The draft start date of 11 March is the proclamation, not the coronation.
* **pe-piero:**
  * The death note is placed at Arundel f. 271b (British Library catalogue) or f. 272r (Museo Galileo).
  * The age of 80 in the note does not match a birth in c.1426.
* **e-1457 / pe-antonio-vinci:** the "1457" catasto was delivered on 28 February 1458. That is the same document, dated Florentine style, so there is no real conflict, but the label should say so.
* **pe-caterina:** three identifications, all recorded as interpretations:
  * Kemp and Pallanti 2017: Caterina di Meo Lippi.
  * Vecce 2023: a Circassian slave freed by an act of 2 November 1452 drawn up by ser Piero. Press reports say this was not yet peer reviewed.
  * Ulivi 2017: other candidates.

  That the Milan Caterina of 1493 to 1494 was his mother is also an interpretation. The funeral expense figures (123 soldi against an itemised sum of about 81) and the folios differ between sources.
* **pe-peers:** the draft says contact with Perugino and Credi is "inferred from workshop context". Both are in fact named with Leonardo in the David committee minutes of 25 January 1504 (Levine 1974). The same applies to Botticelli, Filippino Lippi and Giuliano da Sangallo.
* **pe-predis:** Evangelista died c.1490 to 1491, so the group bar to 1508 is valid for Ambrogio only.
* **pe-gusnasco / pl-mantua:** this round confirmed only "March 1500", not 13 March.
* **pe-novellara:** besides the letter of 3 April 1501 there is a second one, dated 14 April 1501 (the Robertet picture). Isabella's request is dated 27 March 1501 (Mantua archive index).
* **"Mathurin" in the brief:** the will names a woman, "Maturina sua fantesca" (pe-maturina).
* **pe-pompeo-leoni:** died 1608 or 1610.
* **pe-arconati:** the gift is dated 1637 or 1636.
* **Other disputed dates:**
  * pe-raphael, birth day.
  * pe-soderini, born 1450 or 1452.
  * pe-francesco-giocondo, died 1538 or 1539.
  * pe-oggiono, died c.1524 or c.1530.
  * pe-rustici, born 1474 or 1475.
  * pe-giovio, born 1483 or 1486.

## Proposed corrections to the draft (proposals only)
1. pe-peers: change "contact inferred" to "documented co-presence on 25 January 1504 (David committee)". Split into two bars.
2. pe-predis: split into two bars. End Evangelista at c.1490 to 1491.
3. pe-boltraffio and pe-lisa: split into individual bars. Lisa's 1503 date comes from the Vespucci note. That Francesco commissioned the portrait rests on Vasari only.
4. po-leo10: show the election (9 or 11 March, disputed), the proclamation (11 March) and the coronation (19 March) separately.
5. pe-novellara / e-novellara: add the letter of 14 April 1501.
6. e-1457: add "delivered 28 February 1458 modern style".
7. Possible new bars: Antonio da Vinci (1452 to c.1465), Atalante (c.1482, tier T), Battista de Vilanis (1516 to 1519), Agostino Vespucci (October 1503).
