## Conflicts with the first draft
* w-skull: the draft says "a dated inscription marks the drawings" (plural). Only RCIN 919059 is reported as dated ("On the 2nd day of April 1489").
* w-burlington: the National Gallery now dates it c.1506 to 1508. The traditional c.1499 to 1500 rests on Vasari. The draft range of c.1499 to 1508 is fine, but its text should name the National Gallery's current preference.
* w-romorantin: RCT reads the palace on RCIN 912292 as an Amboise island site, not Romorantin. The link to the canal is project context and is not shown on that sheet.
* w-anatomy1: the RCT exhibition texts give winter 1507 to 1508, but one other page gives 1506. The note on the sheet itself, as excerpted, is undated.
* w-baroncelli: the draft basis is "dated inscription on the sheet". No page confirms that the date is on the sheet. It may come from the execution record.
* w-trivulzio-912356: RCT pages disagree (c.1508 to 1510 against c.1510 to 1512).
* e-letter: dates of 1482 to 1483 and c.1483 to 1485 are both in circulation. The hand (Leonardo or a scribe) is unresolved, and the folio was not confirmed.
* Dimensions conflict for w-vitruvian (34.4 x 24.5 against 34.6 x 25.5 cm), w-isabella (61 x 46.5 against 63 x 46 cm, and medium), and w-turin-head (333 x 213 mm against 33.5 x 21.6 cm).
* w-sforza-casting-madrid: the folio is given as 157r by one source and 157v by another.

## Proposed corrections to the chronology (proposals only)
* w-skull text: "One sheet (RCIN 919059) carries the date 2 April 1489; the others are dated by association."
* w-imola: tier S is right. The date (autumn 1502) is the RCT's inference from the Borgia stay at Imola, not from anything written on the sheet.
* w-burlington: add the National Gallery date of c.1506 to 1508 as the current institutional view.
* e-arno: add Landucci's dates for the digging (22 August to 26 October 1504) as cited by RCT, to be checked in the Diario.
