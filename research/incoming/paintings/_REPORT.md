## How the research was done (read first)

* WebFetch could not reach any site in this session. The museum hosts (collections.louvre.fr, nationalgallery.org.uk, nga.gov) and Wikipedia were refused by the session network. All findings come from WebSearch result summaries of the pages named in each claim. Every museum source is therefore typed "other". Its cite says the page was read through a search summary and must be rechecked on the page itself.
* The shared web search budget (200 per turn) ran out partway through. The items it left unresearched are listed below.
* No claim is "verified". Claims that rest only on recall are typed `model_recall` and point to the edition to check. They are: Last Supper medium, the Gallerani letter, the Bellincioni sonnet, the Milanese dating of St Jerome, the 1478 "two Virgins" note for Benois, Gavin Hamilton for the London Rocks, and the Buccleuch theft.
* No Marani 2000 or Zöllner 2003 catalogue numbers were found on any page, so none are given. Every entity lists both catalogues in check_in.
* Inventory numbers are given only where a search summary of the museum's own record stated them. Each one is listed in the next section.

## Conflicts with the first draft, and between sources

* w-carnation: the Munich museum dates it c.1475. The draft has c.1478 to 1480 (c2).
* w-baptist: the Louvre dates it c.1508 to 1519. The draft has c.1513 to 1516 (c2).
* w-baptism: Uffizi pages give c.1470 to 1475 (c3) and 1475 to 1478 (c4). The inventory number appears as both 8358 and 8359 (c2).
* w-magi: the Uffizi names the patrons as Canons Regular of Sant'Agostino. The draft says "monks". The draft's "March 1481" was not confirmed on any page (c2).
* w-rocks: the 1483 contract's delivery deadline is given as 8 December 1483 (c3) and as 8 December 1484 (c4).
* w-musician: dimensions are given as 44.7 x 32 cm (c1) and as 40 x 30 cm (c2). Provenance is given as the Arconati gift of 1637 against the Borromeo gift of 1618 (c7).
* w-litta: one Hermitage page dates it 1490 to 1491 (c2), another to the mid 1490s (c3).
* w-yarnwinder-lansdowne: the Met website gives c.1501 (c2), its wall label c.1505 (c3).
* w-lastsupper: the Pacioli dedication is dated 9 February 1498 by some and the treatise's conclusion 14 December 1497 by others (c4, open question). One secondary source says the commission was in 1494, but no document was seen for it (c10, disputed).
* w-asse: the first Bascapè letter is dated 20 April or 21 April 1498 (c2).
* w-musician, w-ermine, w-salvator: the evidence for the sitter or the attribution is weaker than the draft's wording suggests. Each is recorded as an interpretation claim that names its holder.

## Last Supper handling

The fact layer holds documents only: the ducal memorandum of 29 June 1497 to Marchesino Stanga, Pacioli's mention in the dedication of De divina proportione, Montorfano's Crucifixion on the opposite wall (dated 1495 on the object), and de Beatis in 1517 on the mural's condition. Bandello's dedicatory letter to novella I.58, printed in 1554, is recorded as a later narrative source. Treating it as eyewitness testimony is a separate interpretation claim. The date range is an interpretation claim. Nothing on geometric construction appears.

## Proposed corrections to the draft chronology (proposals only)

1. w-magi: change "monks" to "canons regular", if Villata confirms it. Keep "March 1481" only with a Villata number.
2. w-carnation: give the date range as "c.1475 to 1480", or note the museum's c.1475.
3. w-baptist: widen the range to the museum's c.1508 to 1519, or name who holds c.1513 to 1516.
4. w-rocks: the text "completed later after a legal dispute" should say that the dispute documents date from 1506 to 1508. The London panel now has its own entity, w-rocks-london.
5. w-asse: add "letters of April 1498 (Gualtiero da Bascapè)" to the basis.
6. w-lastsupper: add Pacioli and de Beatis to the basis, and name the 29 June 1497 memorandum.
